# Iceland Space Agency: Public Space Data GUI Project Proposal

This document outlines a comprehensive proposal for a publicly available Graphical User Interface (GUI) designed to visualize public domain space-related data. The project aims to serve as an educational outreach tool for the public while simultaneously demonstrating Iceland's technical capabilities to prospective commercial satellite operators for the ground segment business park.

## 1. Orbital Mechanics & Spacecraft Tracking
*   **Interactive 3D LEO Globe:** A WebGL-based visualization of active satellites, constellations, and trackable debris using real-time Two-Line Element (TLE) sets.
*   **Dynamic Ground Track Plotting:** Tools to map the historical and projected sine-wave footprints of specific spacecraft, featuring a time-scrubber to fast-forward or rewind orbital paths.
*   **Localized Overhead Pass Predictor:** A geolocation widget allowing users to input an Icelandic town or coordinates to receive countdowns and alerts for naked-eye visible satellite passes (e.g., ISS, Hubble).
*   **Deep Space & NEO Monitoring:** Trackers for Near-Earth Object (NEO) close approaches and a live status board of the Deep Space Network (DSN) showing which interplanetary missions are currently downlinking data.
*   **Orbital Mechanics Sandbox:** An educational tool allowing users to manipulate variables like altitude and inclination to observe real-time changes to a satellite's ground track and orbital period.

## 2. Space Weather & Aurora Forecasting (Icelandic Geographic Advantage)
*   **Dynamic Aurora Oval Overlay:** A live, color-coded heatmap of the northern hemisphere displaying the auroral boundary and probability intensity based on the NOAA OVATION model.
*   **Solar Event & CME Tracker:** A visual radar tracking Coronal Mass Ejections (CMEs) and X-class/M-class solar flares, complete with a "Time of Arrival" countdown for Earth's magnetosphere.
*   **High-Latitude Ionosphere Monitor:** Visualizations of ionospheric scintillation and polar cap absorption, highlighting Iceland's strategic vulnerability and research value regarding space weather impacts on high-frequency communications and GPS.
*   **Magnetic Deflection Gauges:** Real-time dashboard metrics for the planetary Kp-index, overlaid with data from regional North Atlantic geomagnetic stations.

## 3. Ground Segment & Infrastructure Visualization
*   **Ground Station Line-of-Sight Visualizer:** A 3D representation of the "data cones" extending from Icelandic ground stations into space. The interface visually indicates when a polar-orbiting satellite enters this cone (Acquisition of Signal) to demonstrate the high contact frequency unique to high-latitude infrastructure.
*   **Business Park Commercial Showcase:** A passive, interactive map highlighting the physical locations of the proposed ground segment business park, partner facilities, and fiber-optic backbone connections, serving as direct outreach to prospective commercial satellite operators.

## 4. Earth Observation & Climate (Remote Sensing)
*   **Icelandic Glacial & Volcanic Monitoring:** Time-lapse sliders utilizing optical imagery to track the retreat of Vatnajökull, combined with Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR/InSAR) overlays to show ground inflation/deflation around active volcanic systems like Fagradalsfjall.
*   **Oceanographic Data Layers:** Visualizations of sea surface temperatures and chlorophyll concentrations around Iceland, demonstrating the downstream economic value of space data for the local fishing and maritime industries.
*   **Thematic Map Overlays:** User-togglable layers for sea ice extent, vegetation indices (NDVI), and thermal anomalies.

## 5. Education, Outreach & Media
*   **"Who is in Space" Counter:** A high-visibility landing metric displaying the current headcount, names, and nationalities of humans off-planet.
*   **CubeSat & University Spotlight:** Dedicated dashboards for small satellites built by academic institutions, detailing mission parameters and battery health.
*   **Launch & Media Feeds:** Integration of live global rocket launch schedules and a searchable gallery of public-domain space imagery and video.
*   **Contextual Glossary:** Hover-over tooltips providing plain-English definitions and visual diagrams for technical terms (e.g., TLE, Right Ascension, Kp-index).

## Verified Data Sources & APIs

### Orbital Data & Tracking
*   **CelesTrak:** The primary public source for TLE sets and SGP4 propagation data.
    *   URL: [https://celestrak.org/](https://celestrak.org/)
*   **Space-Track.org:** The official US Space Command repository for historical and predictive orbital data (requires free registration for API access).
    *   URL: [https://www.space-track.org/](https://www.space-track.org/)
*   **Launch Library 2 API:** Provides comprehensive data on upcoming space launches, rockets, and missions.
    *   URL: [https://thespacedevs.com/llapi](https://thespacedevs.com/llapi)

### Space Weather & Aurora
*   **NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center (SWPC):** Provides JSON/XML feeds for the Kp-index, solar wind speed/density, X-ray flux, and the OVATION aurora forecast model.
    *   URL: [https://www.swpc.noaa.gov/](https://www.swpc.noaa.gov/)
*   **NASA DONKI (Space Weather API):** Detailed data on CMEs, solar flares, and geomagnetic storms.
    *   URL: [https://api.nasa.gov/](https://api.nasa.gov/)

### Earth Observation & Remote Sensing
*   **Copernicus Data Space Ecosystem:** APIs and browser access for Sentinel-1 (Radar) and Sentinel-2 (Optical) imagery.
    *   URL: [https://documentation.dataspace.copernicus.eu/APIs.html](https://documentation.dataspace.copernicus.eu/APIs.html)
*   **NASA Worldview & GIBS:** Global Imagery Browse Services for near-real-time satellite imagery and time-lapse capabilities.
    *   URLs: [https://worldview.earthdata.nasa.gov/](https://worldview.earthdata.nasa.gov/) | [https://gibs.earthdata.nasa.gov/](https://gibs.earthdata.nasa.gov/)
*   **USGS EarthExplorer:** The primary portal for accessing Landsat optical and thermal data.
    *   URL: [https://earthexplorer.usgs.gov/](https://earthexplorer.usgs.gov/)

### Visualization Frameworks (Frontend)
*   **CesiumJS:** The industry-standard open-source JavaScript library for 3D geospatial and orbital visualization in the browser.
    *   URL: [https://cesium.com/cesiumjs/](https://cesium.com/cesiumjs/)
*   **Globe.gl / Three.js:** Alternative WebGL libraries for rendering 3D globes and point-cloud data.
    *   URL: [https://github.com/vasturiano/globe.gl](https://github.com/vasturiano/globe.gl)
