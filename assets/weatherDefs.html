Along-track prediction error
Position error from a decay rate mispredicted by δ: Δs ≈ ¾·n·δ·t², with n in rad/day and t in days Effect: ground-station pointing
475 km, δ = 50 m/day: 3.6 km after 1 day, 14 km after 2 days [calc]. A 6 m dish's X-band beam is 3.7 km wide at 500 km range.

B*

Earth radii⁻¹. ~10⁻⁴ intact objects, ~10⁻³ debris at 450–650 km [FAS]. A fitted value: absorbs model error, can be negative.

Glossary of acronyms
Acronym
Meaning
ACE
Advanced Composition Explorer: a NASA spacecraft monitoring the solar wind at L1
ap, Ap
Planetary equivalent amplitude (ap, every 3 hours) and its daily mean (Ap): linear versions of Kp
AU
Astronomical unit: the mean Sun to Earth distance, about 150 million km
B*
B-star: the drag term in a satellite's orbital element set
CGM
Corrected geomagnetic (coordinates or latitude): the coordinate system that organises aurorae
CIRES
Cooperative Institute for Research in Environmental Sciences (University of Colorado Boulder)
CME, CMEs
Coronal mass ejection(s)
DARPA
Defense Advanced Research Projects Agency (United States)
D-RAP
D-Region Absorption Predictions: NOAA's model of radio absorption in the lowest ionospheric layer
Dst
Disturbance storm time index: the strength of the ring current around Earth, measuring storm depth
ESA
European Space Agency
EUV
Extreme ultraviolet radiation
F10.7, F10.7A
Solar radio flux at a wavelength of 10.7 cm, and its 81-day mean
F2
The upper, denser part of the ionosphere's F layer, whose density peak reflects HF radio
FAS
For All Species: this site
foF2
Critical frequency of the F2 layer: the highest frequency it reflects when the signal is sent straight up
GFZ
GFZ Helmholtz Centre for Geosciences, Potsdam (German Research Centre for Geosciences)
GNSS
Global navigation satellite system(s), such as GPS and Galileo
GOES
Geostationary Operational Environmental Satellite: NOAA's weather satellites, which also measure X-rays and protons
GPS
Global Positioning System
GSM
Geocentric solar magnetospheric: a coordinate system for the interplanetary magnetic field
HF
High frequency radio: 3 to 30 MHz
Hp30, Hp60
Half-hourly and hourly Kp-like geomagnetic indices
HSS
High-speed stream: fast solar wind from a coronal hole
ICAO
International Civil Aviation Organization
IMAP
Interstellar Mapping and Acceleration Probe: a NASA spacecraft that will monitor the solar wind at L1
IMF
Interplanetary magnetic field: the Sun's magnetic field carried outward by the solar wind
ISGI
International Service of Geomagnetic Indices
JB2008
Jacchia-Bowman 2008: a model of the thermosphere's density
Kp
Planetary K index: a 3-hourly measure of geomagnetic disturbance, from 0 to 9
L1
The Sun-Earth Lagrange point about 1.5 million km sunward of Earth, where solar-wind monitors sit
MUF
Maximum usable frequency: the highest frequency the ionosphere reflects over a given path
NCAR
National Center for Atmospheric Research (United States)
NmF2
Peak electron density of the F2 layer
NOAA
National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (United States)
NORAD
North American Aerospace Defense Command
NRLMSIS
Naval Research Laboratory Mass Spectrometer and Incoherent Scatter radar model of the atmosphere
OMM
Orbit mean-elements message: the modern format for a satellite's orbital elements
OVATION
Oval Variation, Assessment, Tracking, Intensity and Online Nowcasting: NOAA's model of the auroral oval
PCA
Polar cap absorption: radio absorption over the poles after a solar particle event
pfu
Particle flux unit: one particle per square centimetre per second per steradian
RF
Radio frequency
ROTI
Rate of TEC index: how fast the electron content changes, a marker of ionospheric irregularities
R / S / G
NOAA's scales for radio blackouts (R), solar radiation storms (S) and geomagnetic storms (G), each from level 1 to 5
S4
Amplitude scintillation index: the strength of signal fading, from 0 to above 1
sfu
Solar flux unit: 10 to the power minus 22 watts per square metre per hertz
SGP4
Simplified General Perturbations 4: the standard model for predicting a satellite's orbit from its element set
SI
Système international: the International System of Units (metres, seconds, kilograms)
SILSO
Sunspot Index and Long-term Solar Observations (Royal Observatory of Belgium)
SN
Sunspot number
SOLAR-1
NOAA's solar-wind monitoring satellite at L1
TEC
Total electron content: the number of electrons in a column through the ionosphere
TECU
TEC unit: 10 to the power 16 electrons per square metre
TLE
Two-line element set: the older format for a satellite's orbital elements
UHF
Ultra high frequency radio: 300 MHz to 3 GHz
UI
University of Iceland
VHF
Very high frequency radio: 30 to 300 MHz
VLEO
Very low Earth orbit: the lowest satellite altitudes, where drag is strongest
